# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person (the owner) learning business and product topics from YouTube without having the time to watch full videos. They read notes at a desk on a laptop, on a phone in short gaps, late at night in a dim room, and listen to the audio version while walking or commuting.

## Product Purpose

YT Notes turns a YouTube link, or a topic, into notes good enough to replace watching: TL;DR, key ideas with timestamps, frameworks, quotes, action items and a glossary of jargon, plus a locally generated Kokoro audio version. Success is reading a one-hour talk in five minutes and trusting that nothing important was missed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, literate. A quiet reader in the lineage of Readwise Reader, Instapaper and Matter: typography first, chrome recedes, nothing competes with the text. Fast to start a new note, effortless to keep listening on the go.

## Anti-references

No strong preference from the owner. Avoid the obvious traps anyway: SaaS dashboard cards and stat tiles, YouTube-style thumbnail grids and red accents, chatbot bubbles and AI sparkle iconography.

## Design Principles

- Reading comes first: comfortable measure, generous leading, quiet chrome.
- Works one-handed on a phone: controls within thumb reach, 44px targets.
- Late night is a first-class context: dark theme is warm and low glare, never pure black.
- Listening continues while browsing: the player is global, not tied to the open note.
- Jargon never blocks understanding: glossary terms are explained in place.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard use, visible focus, respect reduced motion.
